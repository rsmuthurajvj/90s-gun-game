Privacy Policy – 90s Gun Game

Last updated: 7 October 2026

90s Gun Game ("the app", "we", "us") is a free, casual two-player game published for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By installing or using the app you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Offline modes (Pass & Play, vs Computer) work entirely on your device and send us nothing.
  • Play Online sends only a short room code, the nickname you type and the moves you make to our game server. These are deleted automatically within 24 hours.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID.
  • The app is not directed at children under 13.

Information the app handles

1. A nickname you type

You may enter a player name (nickname) to use in a match. In the offline modes it stays on your device. In Play Online it is sent to our game server so your opponent can see who they are playing. Please do not enter your real full name or any sensitive personal information as a nickname.

2. Online match data

When you create or join an online room, our server temporarily stores the room code, the nicknames of the players and the current state of the game (whose turn it is, which soldiers are drawn, the score) so the match can run in real time and survive a brief disconnect. This data is tied to the random room code, not to your identity. Online game rooms and everything in them are deleted automatically within 24 hours of the last move.

3. Data stored only on your device

Your settings (sound, vibration, last-used names and chosen rules), your local win/loss counts, and the code of an online game currently in progress are saved on your device using local storage. We cannot read this data; you can clear it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

4. Advertising and technical data (via Google AdMob)

The app displays ads supplied by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, coarse (country/region-level) location derived from the IP address, device and operating-system information, and general app-usage and ad-interaction information. Where you have given consent, this may include personalised advertising. We ourselves do not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

How the information is used

  • To run online matches in real time and let you reconnect to a game in progress.
  • To remember your settings, names and score on your device.
  • To display ads, keep them relevant, measure their performance and limit how often they appear.
  • To keep the service working, prevent abuse and fix problems.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. Information is processed by the following service providers on our behalf:

  • Google AdMob – serves the ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Our game server host (Render) and database host (MongoDB Atlas) – store the temporary online match data described above while a game is live.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the service.

Data retention

Online match data (room code, nicknames, game state) is automatically deleted within 24 hours of the last move. Data stored on your device remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own policies.

Security

Data sent between the app and our game server is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS/WSS) connection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the limited data we handle.

Your choices and rights

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch the app asks for your consent to personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the app.
  • Personal-data requests: because online match data is anonymous and deleted within 24 hours, there is normally nothing for us to look up. If you believe we hold information about you and would like to access or delete it, contact us at the address below.
  • Playing offline: use Pass & Play or vs Computer to play without sending any data to our server.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

International users

The app is operated from India and uses service providers that may process data in other countries. By using the app you understand that the limited data described here may be processed outside your country of residence.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be reflected here, and continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
